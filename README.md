# policy-management-platform

---

## Problem Statement

ABC's APAC operations team manages insurance policies across several markets in the region. The policy data they work with is spread across spreadsheets, which makes it hard to search, filter, sort and report on consistently. Each market and product line have its own view of the same information, and the team has no single, reliable place to see the state of the portfolio. Spreadsheets are not a suitable and scalable system of record for this work.

---

## Proposed Solution

Replace the spreadsheets with a production-quality platform that gives the operations team:

A platform for viewing, summarizing, searching, retrieving and flagging policies.
 - A summary statistics panel view that reports figures correctly, in particular premiums per line of business and per currency, never summed across currencies.
 - A Dashboard with a paginated policy table, filtering and sorting, bulk flag-for-review, and light and dark themes.
